KML to GeoJSON converter

Move places, routes and areas drawn in Google Earth or My Maps to a web map or a GIS tool.

Accepts

KML map file (.kml). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. Every placemark is read, in folders too: points, lines, polygons with their holes, multi-geometries and gx:Track tracks with their times.
  2. Names, descriptions and extended data become properties. Styles and icons are not carried over. A KMZ is a zipped KML: unzip it first (Unzip is in File convert).

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Can I get a GPX instead?

Yes. KML to GPX is its own page, through GeoJSON.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
