SVG to JPG converter

Rasterise an SVG to a JPG for apps that do not take vector files. The browser draws it at your size, so edges stay sharp.

Accepts

SVG image (.svg). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. The SVG is drawn by this browser at the width or height you choose; with neither, its own size is used (1024 px on the long side when it has only a viewBox).
  2. JPG has no transparency, so transparent areas are filled with the background colour, white by default.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Why choose a size?

An SVG has no fixed pixel size, so the JPG needs one. Pick the size the picture will be shown at.

JPG or PNG?

PNG keeps transparency and sharp flat colours. JPG is smaller for pictures with gradients and photos.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
