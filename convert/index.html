Images to PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP and ICO, and to PDF. PDF pages to images, and PDF merge and split. CSV to JSON, JSON to CSV, CSV to Excel. Markdown to HTML, HTML to text, Base64 and URL encoding, and line endings. Each converter also has its own page.

Batches work: drop as many files as you like. Converted files download one at a time, or as one zip.
