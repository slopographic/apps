MP3 to WAV converter

Turn an MP3 into a WAV file for an audio editor, a sampler or a CD.

Accepts

MP3 audio (.mp3). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. The MP3 is decoded by this browser and written as 16-bit PCM WAV at 44.1 kHz, stereo or mono as the MP3 has it.
  2. No engine download is needed.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Does WAV sound better than the MP3?

No. WAV keeps exactly what the MP3 decodes to; it cannot bring back what MP3 compression removed.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
