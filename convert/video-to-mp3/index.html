Video to MP3 converter

Take the sound out of a video and save it as an MP3 file, for a podcast, a voiceover or a song.

How it works

The video is read in this browser and its audio track is decoded. The picture is not used.

The sound is encoded as an MP3 at the bitrate you choose. Stereo videos stay stereo, mono stays mono.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

Does the video need a sound track?

Yes. A video with no sound track gives a clear message instead of an empty file.

Which bitrate should I pick?

128 kbps is fine for speech. 192 or 320 kbps keeps more detail in music.

Are video files uploaded?

No. The decoding and the MP3 encoding both run in your browser. Large videos take longer and use more memory, since the sound is decoded in one go.
