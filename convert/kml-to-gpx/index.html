KML to GPX converter

Plan a route in Google Earth or My Maps, then load it into a GPS watch or a hiking app.

Accepts

KML map file (.kml). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. Step 1 reads every placemark of the KML into GeoJSON: points, lines, polygons and tracks, with names and descriptions.
  2. Step 2 writes GPX 1.1: points become waypoints and lines become tracks. An area has no GPX form, so its outline becomes a track, and the status line counts them.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Can I keep the GeoJSON made in between?

Yes. Open Options and tick Also keep the in-between files: it is listed with the result.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
