Cut video: trim MP4, MOV and WebM clips

Keep only the part of a video you want to share. The clip stays in the same format as the video.

Accepts

MP4 video (.mp4), QuickTime video (.mov), WebM video (.webm). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. Type the start and the end in seconds or minutes and seconds; a blank end keeps the rest of the video.
  2. By default the picture and sound are copied as they are: fast, and nothing is lost, but the clip starts at the key frame just before the start. Tick Cut at the exact frame to re-encode instead (slower).
  3. The video engine (ffmpeg.wasm) is downloaded once the first time, after the page asks; then the browser keeps it.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

What is the largest video?

400 MB. The whole video has to fit in the browser’s memory.

Why does my clip start a little early?

Copying can only start at a key frame. Tick Cut at the exact frame for a cut to the exact moment.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
