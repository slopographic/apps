Word to PDF converter

No Word on this computer? Drop the document here and get a PDF that prints and shares the way it should, made in your browser.

Accepts

Word documents (.docx). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. Drop one or more Word documents (.docx). Each becomes its own PDF; download them one by one or all as a ZIP.
  2. The file is read by the Slop Docs engine: paragraphs, headings, bold, italic, underline, colours, fonts, lists, tables, links, page size, margins, headers and footers. Pictures, footnotes, comments and tracked changes are not carried over yet.
  3. Text in Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese, Hindi and other writing systems is set with a Noto font for that script, downloaded once after the page asks.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Do I need Microsoft Word?

No. The document is read and laid out in your browser by the Slop Docs engine, so it works on any computer, tablet or phone with a modern browser.

What about old .doc and .ppt files?

Those older binary formats are not read here. Save them as .docx or .pptx in Word, PowerPoint or LibreOffice first.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
