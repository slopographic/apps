FLAC to MP3 converter

Make MP3 copies of FLAC music for devices that do not play FLAC.

Accepts

FLAC audio (.flac). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. The FLAC file is decoded by this browser and encoded as MP3 with LAME, on this page.
  2. 320 kbps keeps nearly all of what most people can hear; 192 kbps saves more space.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Are the tags kept?

No. This conversion writes the sound only, so add the title and artist in your music app.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
