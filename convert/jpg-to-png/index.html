JPG to PNG converter

Convert JPG photos to PNG for editing, sharing or a lossless archive. PNG keeps every pixel exactly as the decoder read it.

How it works

The JPG is decoded in this browser and drawn onto a canvas, then saved as PNG.

A JPG cannot regain detail lost when it was first compressed, so the PNG is larger but no more accurate than the JPG.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

Will converting to PNG improve image quality?

No. PNG stores the pixels JPG already has. It stops further quality loss if you edit and save again.

Does EXIF rotation survive?

The image is drawn upright, so the PNG shows the photo the right way round.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
