Unit converter: length, weight, time, data, temperature

Convert between metric, imperial and data units. Decimal inputs are worked out exactly, so 1 mile is exactly 1.609344 km.

How it works

Each value is read as an exact decimal and converted with exact fractions. An answer is shown exactly when it has a finite decimal form, and rounded to 12 places with ≈ when it does not.

Temperature conversions run through kelvin, so the offsets are exact too.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

Why exact rather than floating point?

Binary floating point cannot store 0.1 exactly, so small errors creep in. Exact arithmetic gives the answer you would work out by hand.

Which data units are there?

Bytes, kilobytes, megabytes and so on in powers of 1000, and kibibytes, mebibytes and so on in powers of 1024.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
