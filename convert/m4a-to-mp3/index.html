M4A to MP3 converter

Turn M4A files, such as iPhone voice memos, into MP3s that every player and app accepts.

Accepts

M4A audio (.m4a). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. The M4A (AAC) sound is decoded by this browser and encoded as MP3 with LAME, on this page.
  2. Pick the bitrate: 128 kbps is fine for voice memos, 192 or 320 kbps for music.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Does it work with protected iTunes files?

No. Files with copy protection (DRM) cannot be decoded. Purchases from the iTunes Store since 2009 have no DRM and work.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
