PNG to JPG converter

Turn PNG pictures into JPG files. JPG has no transparency, so transparent areas are filled with the background colour you choose.

How it works

Drop one or many PNG files. Each one is decoded by this browser, drawn onto a canvas and encoded as JPG at the quality you pick.

Transparent pixels take the background colour, white by default. Choose a size to resize while keeping the proportions.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

Why does my transparent PNG get a background?

JPG cannot store transparency. Every transparent pixel is filled with the background colour so the result looks the same on any screen.

What quality should I choose?

90 is a good default. Lower values make smaller files with visible artefacts; 100 gives the largest file.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
