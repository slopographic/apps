PNG to PDF converter

Turn one or many PNG images into a single PDF. Transparency is flattened onto white, because a printed page has no transparency.

How it works

Each PNG is flattened onto white and placed on its own page, centred and scaled to the margins you choose.

Use Fit to each image to make every page the size of its picture.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

Why is the background white?

A PDF page prints on paper. Transparent areas become white so the page looks right.

Can I put several PNGs in one PDF?

Yes. Add all the files at once and they become one PDF, one image per page, in the order you added them.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
