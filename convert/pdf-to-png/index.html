PDF to PNG converter

Save PDF pages as PNG pictures, one file per page. Choose the pages and the resolution.

How it works

The PDF is rendered by the PDF.js engine in this browser. Each chosen page is drawn at the resolution you pick and saved as PNG.

Leave Pages blank for all pages, or write a list such as 1-3, 7.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

What resolution should I pick?

144 dpi suits screens. 216 dpi suits printing and close reading. Higher values make bigger files.

Can I open a password-protected PDF?

Not here. Unlock the PDF in a PDF app first.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
