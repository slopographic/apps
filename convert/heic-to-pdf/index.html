HEIC to PDF converter

Send a set of iPhone photos as one PDF: drop them all, and each becomes a page.

Accepts

HEIC and HEIF photos (.heic, .heif), as iPhones save them. Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. Each HEIC photo is decoded (by Safari itself, or by the HEIC decoder, which the page asks for first) and saved as a JPG, which is placed in the PDF as it is.
  2. Choose A4, US Letter or Fit to each image, and the margin. Switch the tile in the middle to PNG to keep every pixel, at the cost of a larger PDF.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Are all the photos in one PDF?

Yes. Every photo you drop is a page of the same PDF, in the order of the list.

Can I keep the file made in between?

Yes. Open Options and tick Also keep the in-between files: it is listed with the result.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Both steps run in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
