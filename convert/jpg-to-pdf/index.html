JPG to PDF converter

Combine JPG photos into one PDF for printing or sending. Each photo gets a page, with a margin you can set.

How it works

JPG photos are embedded in the PDF without re-compression when they are upright. Photos with a sideways EXIF rotation are drawn upright first.

Choose A4, Letter, or fit each page to its photo.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

Is the photo quality reduced?

Upright JPGs are embedded as they are, so their quality does not change. Rotated photos are re-encoded at high quality.

Why do my photos show sideways?

Phones store rotation as metadata. This converter applies it so the page shows the photo the right way up.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
