PowerPoint to PDF converter

No PowerPoint on this computer? Drop the presentation here and get a PDF of the slides, made in your browser.

Accepts

PowerPoint presentations (.pptx). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. Drop one or more .pptx files. Each becomes its own PDF; download them one by one or all as a ZIP.
  2. The file is read by the Slop Slides engine: text, shapes, pictures, tables, backgrounds and the theme. Charts, SmartArt, video and sound are left out, and the status line counts them.
  3. Text in Arabic, Hebrew, Chinese, Hindi and other writing systems is set with a Noto font for that script, downloaded once after the page asks.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Do I need Microsoft PowerPoint?

No. The deck is read and drawn by the Slop Slides engine in your browser, so it works without Office.

What about old .doc and .ppt files?

Those older binary formats are not read here. Save them as .docx or .pptx in Word, PowerPoint or LibreOffice first.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
