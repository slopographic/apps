HTML to plain text converter

Get the readable text of a web page or HTML file, without tags, scripts or styles.

How it works

The HTML is parsed by this browser. Scripts, styles and the head are removed, and paragraphs, headings and list items become lines.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

Are the links kept?

No. Only the visible text is kept. The link text stays, the addresses do not.

Does it run the scripts?

No. Scripts are removed and never run.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
