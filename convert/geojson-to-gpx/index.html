GeoJSON to GPX converter

Load a route drawn on geojson.io or exported from QGIS into a GPS watch or a navigation app.

Accepts

GeoJSON (.geojson). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. Points and multi-points become waypoints with their name, description and time. Lines become tracks; a multi-line becomes one track with a segment per line.
  2. Elevation comes from the third coordinate and point times from coordTimes. Areas have no GPX form, so their outlines are written as tracks.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Does a .json file work?

Rename it to .geojson first. A file ending in .json is treated as plain JSON and offered the JSON conversions.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
