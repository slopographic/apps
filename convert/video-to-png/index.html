Video to PNG converter (a frame)

Take a still picture from a video as a PNG: lossless, so the frame keeps every detail the video had.

How it works

The video is read by ffmpeg.wasm, the frame at the time you choose is decoded, and the picture is saved as PNG.

PNG is lossless and larger than JPG or WebP.

Your files never leave this device. The video engine is downloaded once from jsDelivr the first time you convert a video, then the browser keeps it.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

When should I use PNG rather than JPG?

When you want to edit the frame, or keep every detail. JPG is smaller.

Which frame do I get?

The frame at the time you enter, in seconds.

Are files uploaded?

No. The frame is taken on this device.
