Split PDF: extract pages or one file per page

Pull pages out of a PDF, or split it into one file per page. Everything happens on this device.

How it works

Enter a range such as 2-4, 9 to make one PDF with those pages. Leave it blank to get one PDF per page, downloadable as a zip.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and the converter runs entirely in your browser.

Questions

How do I write a page range?

Use commas and dashes: 1-3, 5, 8- means pages 1 to 3, page 5, and page 8 to the end.

Is the original changed?

No. The original file is read and new PDFs are made from its pages.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Every conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
