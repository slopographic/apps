EPUB to PDF converter

Print a book, read it in a PDF reader, or send it to someone without an e-reader.

Accepts

EPUB book (.epub). Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. Step 1 reads the chapters in reading order and writes them as Markdown, so headings, bold, italic, lists and quotes keep their meaning.
  2. Step 2 lays the Markdown out on pages with the Slop Docs engine: choose A4 or US Letter and a sans, serif or monospaced font. The text in the PDF is real text you can search and copy.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Can I convert a book from a store?

Only if it has no DRM. Books locked with DRM keep their text encrypted, so the page says so and converts nothing.

Are the pictures in the PDF?

No, they become their description in brackets. Tables stay as the Markdown text of the table.

Can I keep the Markdown?

Yes. Open Options and tick Also keep the in-between files.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
