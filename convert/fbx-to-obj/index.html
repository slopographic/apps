FBX to OBJ converter

Get an OBJ from an FBX file for tools that do not read FBX. The parts keep their names and their places.

Accepts

FBX 3D model (.fbx, binary or ASCII). Other file types are not converted on this page.

How to convert

  1. Drop the .fbx file, with any texture files it names.
  2. Each part is written in world space as its own OBJ object, with its material in the .mtl file.
  3. After converting, the status line gives the triangle and vertex counts, the size, and what the new format could not hold.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

What happens to animations?

OBJ cannot hold them. The model is written in the pose it stands in, and the status line lists what was left out.

Are the textures saved?

Colour textures are saved as PNG files and named in the MTL file. Download the files together as a zip.

Does it read ASCII FBX?

Yes, binary and ASCII FBX 7.0 and newer.
