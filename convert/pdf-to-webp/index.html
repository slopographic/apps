PDF to WebP converter

Get the pages of a PDF as small WebP pictures for a website or a chat.

Accepts

PDF documents (.pdf). Password-protected PDFs are refused. Other file types are not converted on this page; the File convert hub takes every kind it knows.

How to convert

  1. Each page is drawn as a picture at the resolution you choose (step 1); type a page range to get only some pages.
  2. Each picture is then saved as WebP (step 2) with the quality and size you choose. Download them one by one or all as a ZIP.

Your files stay on this device. Nothing is uploaded: the conversion runs in your browser, and the file is never sent to Slopographic or to any other server.

Questions

Why WebP?

WebP pictures are often a third smaller than PNG or JPG at the same look, and every current browser shows them.

Can I keep the file made in between?

Yes. Open Options and tick Also keep the in-between files: it is listed with the result.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Both steps run in your browser, on this device. Your files are not sent to Slopographic or to any other server.
